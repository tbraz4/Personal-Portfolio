PROJECT #3


Third Project is a Twine game. I created a simple twine game where a person asks you how the weather is and you have three ways to respond. You can respond in a nice way where you comment on the weather saying that its nice, a normal responds would be you saying that it may get worse later on or unsure, and the third responds is being rude as if you were in a bad mood and you take it out on the person who was simply asking you one questions.
